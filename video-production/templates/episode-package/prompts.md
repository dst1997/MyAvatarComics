# AI Prompt Pack

## Style Bible

Soft 3D storybook animation for preschool children. Bright natural colors,
rounded friendly shapes, warm daylight, simple uncluttered backgrounds,
gentle facial expressions, soft camera movement, no scary shadows, no brand
logos, no text on screen unless explicitly required, no copyrighted characters,
no real-person likenesses.

## Runway Prompt Formula

Use this structure for each shot:

```text
Soft 3D storybook animation for preschool children. [Original character names
and visual descriptions]. [Location]. [Action in one simple sentence]. Gentle
camera movement: [push in / pan / static / follow]. Mood: warm, safe, calm,
playful. Keep faces kind and readable. No logos, no text, no copyrighted
characters, no scary imagery, no product packaging.
```

## ElevenLabs Narrator Direction

Warm adult storyteller voice. Slow, gentle pacing. Smile in the voice. Clear
preschool pronunciation. No exaggerated excitement. Leave small pauses between
sentences.

## Music Direction

Gentle, cheerful, soft storybook background music. Light marimba, ukulele,
soft piano, or bells. No lyrics. No dramatic tension. Low volume under narrator.

## Sound Effects Direction

Use only small, friendly effects: soft footsteps, toy blocks, page turn, tiny
sparkle, gentle outdoor birds, or soft room tone. Avoid startling sounds.
