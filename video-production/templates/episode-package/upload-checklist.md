# Upload Checklist

## Story QA

- [ ] Runtime is 60-90 seconds.
- [ ] Narration is 90-140 words.
- [ ] Story has a clear beginning, middle, and end.
- [ ] The episode teaches one positive life skill.
- [ ] The final takeaway is simple and kind.

## Preschool Safety QA

- [ ] Vocabulary is simple and everyday.
- [ ] No scary conflict, jump scares, or intense danger.
- [ ] No bullying, humiliation, insults, or shame.
- [ ] No unsafe behavior shown as fun or repeatable.
- [ ] No shopping focus or product promotion.
- [ ] No call to like, subscribe, comment, click, buy, download, or ask parents to buy.
- [ ] No copyrighted characters, brands, logos, or real child likenesses.

## AI Clip QA

- [ ] Characters are visually consistent enough across shots.
- [ ] No confusing AI artifacts in faces, hands, props, or motion.
- [ ] No accidental text, logos, watermarks, or brand-like marks.
- [ ] Audio is clear and gentle.
- [ ] Music does not overpower narration.

## Export QA

- [ ] MP4 export plays start to finish.
- [ ] 1920x1080, 16:9.
- [ ] H.264 video, 30 fps.
- [ ] AAC stereo audio, 48 kHz.
- [ ] Thumbnail accurately represents the video.

## YouTube Studio

- [ ] Upload manually in YouTube Studio.
- [ ] Set audience to Made for Kids.
- [ ] Review AI-use disclosure and mark accurately.
- [ ] Confirm no paid product placement unless intentionally disclosed.
- [ ] Review title and description for honesty and no keyword stuffing.
